Felt Piano Practice Tips for Classical & Cathedral

Whether you are preparing a calm recital piece, building finger independence on Bach's BWV 846 arpeggios, or mastering the nocturnal rubato of Chopin Op. 9 No. 2 and Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata, each score page includes the real key signature, tempo marking, chord map, playable keyboard range, and a direct falling-notes tutorial on YouTube @rickthepianist.

  • For Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata (C# minor) and Bach's Prelude in C Major, practice blocked chords first so your hand memorizes every harmonic transition before playing broken arpeggios.
  • On Chopin Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 and Satie Gymnopédie No. 1, keep the left-hand waltz bass feather-light so the cantabile right-hand melody floats effortlessly.
  • Use subtle phrase-end rubato (slight breath before climaxes) rather than erratic speed swings—steady underlying pulse creates authentic classical poise.
